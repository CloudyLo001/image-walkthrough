# On-screen hooks

The hook is the text burned into the video. It is **not** the caption: the caption is a short reaction posted under the video, while the hook sets up the scene the viewer is about to walk through.

## Look

- **TikTok Sans, TikTok's default text font, in white with a black outline.** This is fixed; don't use other fonts or styles.
- Centred on the footage, not on the whole 9:16 frame.
- Mostly lowercase. Proper nouns keep their capitals ("Kim's house then").
- 1–4 short lines. An optional second thought goes in brackets on its own line:
  "the great wolf lodge is the closest thing we have to backrooms / (look up great wolf lodge at night)".
- Shown for the whole video.

## Formulas that worked, with real examples from the top posts

## Rules for writing new hooks

1. Name a **specific** person, era, place or feeling. "a late summer night in 2020" beats "summer".
2. Make the viewer place themselves in it: pov, family role, class, school age, a celebrity they know.
3. Leave a gap the room fills. The words set it up, the world pays it off.
4. Give 2 options per idea that use different formulas, so the A/B choice is a real choice.
5. It's fine to reuse a proven hook with a new place or person. Change the noun, keep the shape.
