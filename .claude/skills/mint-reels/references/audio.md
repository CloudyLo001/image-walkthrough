# Audio

Audio is always the **last** editing step, chosen once the picture is locked.

## What top posts used (TikTok)

- Nostalgic or emo rock: The Diary of Jane by Breaking Benjamin (7.9M), "resonance" midwest emo (898K)
- Liminal or dreamy ambient: Six Forty Seven by Instupendo (1.6M), The Backrooms Sound (3.8M)
- Old jazz for period rooms: In a Sentimental Mood by Benny Goodman (2M, Victorian homes)
- Trending "original sound" clips from other creators (28.3M, 19M, 6.4M)

## Suggesting 3–5 options

1. At least one sound trending right now, from the daily research.
2. At least one sound that matches the era or mood of the place (jazz for Victorian, early-2000s pop for y2k, ambient for liminal).
3. At least one proven sound from the table above, when it fits.
4. For each option give: the title, the artist or creator, a link, why it fits, and where the drop or best 8–10 s starts.

## Rules

- Cut the video to the beat only after the picture is locked. Align the drop to the first cut or to the reveal.
- A sound baked in from YouTube can get the video muted, so always export the silent copy too.
- Business accounts can only use TikTok's Commercial Music Library when adding sound in the app. Check that before choosing a popular song.
