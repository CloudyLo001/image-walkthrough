# Shot grammar

Every clip is a **camera path**, recorded live and then rendered offline at full detail. Only the path is saved, so a clip can be re-rendered or changed later.

## Moves

| Move | What it is | Raw length | Typical speed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Push-in | Walk straight toward the focal point of the room | 3–5 s | 2–3.5× |
| Slow pan | Stand still and turn 60–120° across the room | 3–4 s | 2–3× |
| Walkthrough | Move through a doorway or down a hall into the next space | 4–6 s | 3–4× |
| Rise-reveal | Start low near furniture and rise to eye level | 3–4 s | 2× |
| Orbit | Arc around a centrepiece such as a bed, table or tub | 4–5 s | 2–3× |

## Picking shots

- Start from the most recognisable angle of the room, the view someone would photograph.
- Stay inside the good part of the splat. Avoid edges where the world gets soft or smeared, and floors or ceilings close up.
- Stay at eye level (about 1.6 m) unless the move is a rise-reveal.
- Pick 4–6 candidates per world and keep the 2–3 best.
- When judging stills: sharpness first, then a clear subject, then lighting and colour.

## Aspect

- Recording 16:9 gives the letterboxed look of most top posts. Recording 9:16 gives a full-screen reel.
- Choose one per reel, following `edit-rules.md`.
