# Themes and idea mix

## Theme buckets, ranked by what has worked

1. **Celebrity and famous houses:** Kim K's old and new house, North's room, Drake's houses, Marie Antoinette, Barbie's house. (28.3M, 6.4M, 3.8M, 5.3M)
2. **Dream rooms tied to a family role or life stage:** the eldest daughter moving out, the youngest child, the older sister in purple, a nursery "i hope I have a girl someday", a future apartment. (10.7M, 19M, 4.9M)
3. **Childhood and school nostalgia:** a kindergarten, a school container classroom, graduation, Great Wolf Lodge, Golden Corral, Toys R Us, Publix, Hawaii 2006, a 2020 summer bedroom. (3.1M, 1.9M, 1.6M, 1M)
4. **Liminal, backrooms, dreamcore and weirdcore:** hospitals, endless hotels, sick dreams, repeating dream worlds. (5.1M, 3.2M, 1.5M)
5. **History and far-off places:** the Library of Alexandria, the Colosseum, Victorian homes, old churches, a Japanese machiya. (2M, 1.9M, 983K)
6. **Class, era and aesthetic identity:** middle-lower class, Tuscan, industrial loft, y2k, Frutiger Aero, Vector Flourish, 2000s movies. (7.9M, 723K)
7. **Relatable humour:** office "quick question", cookie-cutter suburbs, IKEA dates. (smaller, but cheap to make)

## The mix for each daily batch of up to 20 ideas

- About 8 **proven**: a bucket and formula that already did well, remixed with a new subject, e.g. "id rather have <another celebrity>'s old house".
- About 6 **older**: a theme we have done before that hasn't run in over 30 days, or an older format worth another try.
- About 6 **new**: a theme or place we have never done, ideally tied to something trending this week.

Each idea card lists:
- the title
- its bucket and tag (`proven`, `older` or `new`)
- the past post it remixes, if any
- 2–4 rooms, each with a one-line visual
- 2 draft hooks
- 1 draft caption
- `audioHints`
- a credit estimate: rooms × the Mint world cost

## Reuse rule

Reusing a winning topic, hook or caption is encouraged, but always change the subject, the place or the wording. Never post the same text twice.
