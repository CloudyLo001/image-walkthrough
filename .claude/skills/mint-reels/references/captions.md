# Captions and hashtags

## Caption

- **3–9 words**, mostly lowercase, first person, as if you had just seen the video yourself.
- Tone: a wistful, dry or playful reaction. Never describe what is on screen.
- Then **4–5 hashtags**. Nothing else: no emojis at the start, no links, no calls to action.

Real examples from the top posts:
- her house will not be a mess
- i could never live here
- id rather have kim k's old house
- for now it'll only exist on my computer :(
- I can't tell if I'm scared or relaxed
- this would be a long dream
- and i never saw them again after graduation
- super stinky royalty
- the sprinkle cookies iykyk
- i wish I was making this up

Patterns: `id rather have <celebrity>'s old house`, `i could never <do X> here`, `i miss <time>`, `<place> was literally like this`, `i wish they were`, `if only i knew how`.

## Hashtags

Pick 4–5: 1–2 from the core set and 2–3 about the topic.

- **Core set:** #roomdesign #roombuild #rareaesthetic #nostalgia #gamer #design #worldbuild #girlythings #dreambedroom
- **Topic tags** name the specific thing: #kimk #greatwolflodge #publix #victorian #colosseum #backrooms #liminalspaces #y2k #eldestdaughter #kindergarten #frutigeraero

## Per platform

- **TikTok:** caption and hashtags exactly as written. Add the sound in the app from the silent export.
- **YouTube Shorts:** the title is the caption and hashtags (100 characters or fewer). Description can be empty.
- **Instagram:** the same caption and hashtags. The cover is `cover.jpg`.
